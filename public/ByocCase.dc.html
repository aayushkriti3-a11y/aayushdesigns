02 · PREP

Why BYOC, and why on MCSP

Customers wanted a managed service without their data leaving their own cloud. The Multi-cloud SaaS Platform (MCSP) already had the shape for that: one part IBM runs, one part the customer owns.

CONTROL PLANE · IBM RUNS IT Service orchestration, platform updates, lifecycle operations and overall service health. One consistent management layer.
DATA PLANE · THE CUSTOMER OWNS IT Runs inside the customer's own AWS account and connects securely to IBM's control plane, so the data stays in their environment.
THE TAKEAWAY MCSP provides the shared platform and architecture. InnerSource lets product teams like AIOps reuse and extend it, instead of building their own.
